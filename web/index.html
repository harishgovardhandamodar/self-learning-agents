Landscape of self-learning agents

    
The academic and industry field, placed on the axes this lab
      measures. The source facts (title, venue, what it does) are what the
      work states about itself; the alignment columns are this lab's
      assessment, and are marked as such.

    


    

      
      
      
      
      
    


    

      
      
      
      
      
    


    

      
One row per work, one column per axis. A work whose protocol
        measures nothing under drift cannot have its own claims falsified —
        that is the last column.

      

    


    

      

        

          
The landscape: the mechanisms, and the works that implement them

          
Each mechanism heads a box, and
            the works that implement it sit below it. An edge means the work
            implements that mechanism — it is not a citation.

        

        

          
        

      

      

      

    


    

      

        

          
Mechanisms grouped by what they rewrite

            kind improves, this is what it rewrites.
